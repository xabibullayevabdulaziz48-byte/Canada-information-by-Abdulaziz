Official Symbols

What is Canada's National Animal?

Canada actually has two official national animals representing different eras of its heritage:

Official Emblem Since 1975

The North American Beaver (Castor canadensis)

The beaver is Canada's quintessential animal symbol. During early exploration and settlement, the fur trade drew explorers westward and built Canada's early economy. Celebrated for industriousness and resilience.

Featured on the Canadian 5-cent coin (nickel) and Canada's first postage stamp in 1851!
Official National Horse (2002)

The Canadian Horse (Cheval Canadien)

Recognized officially in 2002, the Canadian Horse descends from royal French horses sent to New France by King Louis XIV in the 17th century. Known as the "little iron horse" for its endurance and strength.

Admired for its gentle temperament, dark coat, and historic contribution to Canadian agriculture.
